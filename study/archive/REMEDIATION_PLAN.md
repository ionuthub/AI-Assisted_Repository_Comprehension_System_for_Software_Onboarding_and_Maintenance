# Archived remediation plan

This plan followed the independent audit. It is historical and has been superseded by the current artefact and study records.

## Priority 0: fix measurement defects

The plan required fixes for:

- incorrect path extraction;
- incomplete generations;
- unreadable files remaining in the index;
- long-line excerpt selection;
- code-related stopwords;
- overconfident no-evidence wording;
- misleading coverage wording;
- gate scripts that could succeed with incomplete data;
- marking sheets not bound strongly enough to their gate.

## Priority 1: recapture and re-mark

After fixing measurement defects:

1. capture both study repositories again;
2. mark all 24 answers;
3. collect verdicts into the gate files;
4. calculate the final figure from the scripts;
5. freeze the artefact before participant collection.

## Priority 2: correct documentation

The audit required the AI disclosure, protocol and gate instructions to match what was actually done, including tool-verified reference answers and machine-produced second marking.

## Priority 3: run the participant study

The remaining research work was recruitment, counterbalancing, participant sessions, analysis and the dissertation write-up.

## Future work

Improvements such as AST-level chunks, hybrid retrieval, graph expansion, reranking, larger indexing and evidence-constrained generation were left for future work because changing retrieval during the study would invalidate the frozen measurements.
